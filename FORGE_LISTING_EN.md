## Description
Are you tired of your Scav showing up late to a raid? Sick of missing those pesky PMCs who arrive early just to STEAL your priceless trash?

**NOT ANYMORE!**

Introducing **ScavTime™** — a revolutionary new way to get your Scav out of bed and into the raid ON TIME!

ScavTime disables SPT's random late Scav spawn timer, giving your Scav the full standard raid time from the start. No more randomly joining with half the clock already gone. It's just you, your precious loot, and a bunch of hot-headed PMCs armed to the teeth and ready to kill you.

**Features**
- Full standard starting raid time for player Scavs.
- Works server-side; no client plugin or F12 setup.
- PMC raid duration, loot, bot spawns, and extractions are not modified.
- Designed for SPT 4.1.6; Fika shared-backend compatibility (live co-op testing pending).
- Fika Headless is optional, not required.

Have fun, and always remember… **NO REFUNDS!**
